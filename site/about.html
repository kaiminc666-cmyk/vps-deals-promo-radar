About this site

vps-deals is an independently operated VPS price monitoring project, maintained by its site operator.

What we do

We monitor VPS hosting offers and make it easier to compare the prices that providers publish on their own websites.

A price is shown only when it can be read from structured data on a provider's official public page. We do not invent prices, discounts, or renewal terms. When a price is missing, unclear, or expired, we leave it out and link readers to the provider's source so they can confirm current terms.

This site is an informational comparison resource. A listing is not a recommendation, and the provider's website controls its products, availability, and billing terms.

Prices and availability can change. Always confirm terms on the provider's official page.

Sources are linked on every listing. Missing or ambiguous prices are omitted.

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
