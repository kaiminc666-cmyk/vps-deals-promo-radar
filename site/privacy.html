Privacy on this site

This page explains the services used by vps-deals and what to expect when you follow a link away from this site.

Cloudflare analytics and delivery

This site uses Cloudflare hosting and Web Analytics. Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation says its performance beacon collects timing metrics to report page performance and that Cloudflare does not track individual end users across its customers' Internet properties. Cloudflare may also process technical request information as part of delivering and protecting website traffic. See Cloudflare's data collection documentation and its privacy policy for details.

Third-party links, advertising, and affiliate links

This site may display third-party advertisements and may include affiliate links. If you follow a link, the destination website operates under its own privacy policy and decides what information it collects. We do not control those sites or their data practices; review the destination's policy before sharing information there.

If you purchase through an affiliate link, the site may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. The link's destination may use its own cookies or other tracking tools under its own policy.

Messages to the contact address

If you email us, we receive the sender address and any information you include in your message so the site operator can read and respond to it. Do not include information you do not want to share by email.

Prices and availability can change. Always confirm terms on the provider's official page.

Sources are linked on every listing. Missing or ambiguous prices are omitted.

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
